02 Academic work

Research & study

Questions about security, systems, and everyday life.

I completed an MSc in Cybersecurity at the University of West London in September 2024.

MSc dissertation

Cybersecure Integration of Embedded Systems in IoT: Safeguarding Connectivity and Data Integrity

This dissertation was a qualitative literature review and case-study-based study of security in connected embedded systems.

It explored lightweight cryptography, secure connectivity, data integrity, IoT protocols, and AI/ML security approaches.

The work was research and analysis; it did not involve empirical testing or the development of a working security product.

Undergraduate research

Public transport and urban mobility in Kathmandu

This research explored overcrowding and inefficiencies in Kathmandu’s public transport system.

It considered a proposed mobile application for routes, estimated arrival times, and crowding information. The application was a proposed solution, not a completed, functional app.
