Privacy & Cookies

1. About this website

This website, chonang.com.np, is operated by Chonang Rai.

For questions about this website or its privacy practices, contact:

chonang@chonang.com.np

2. Information this website collects

This website is a personal website and portfolio. It presents information about its operator, including academic background, research interests, projects and personal interests.

The website does not currently provide visitor accounts, contact forms, analytics, advertising or user-submission features.

If you contact me by email, I will receive the information you choose to include in your message, such as your email address, name and message content. I use this information to read and respond to your enquiry.

3. Hosting and technical information

The website is delivered through Cloudflare and hosted using Render.

When you visit a website, hosting and infrastructure providers may process technical information required to deliver, maintain and protect the service. This may include IP addresses, browser information, requested pages, timestamps and security-related request data.

I do not operate a visitor-tracking or analytics system on this website. I cannot independently determine all technical information processed or retained by the infrastructure providers, or their applicable retention periods. Please also consult their respective privacy information.

4. Cookies and similar technologies

The current website does not intentionally use analytics, advertising or tracking cookies.

During a review of the live website on 1 November 2026, no application cookies were observed in the responses from its public pages or static assets.

The website uses Django's session and security middleware, but the current public pages do not appear to set session or CSRF cookies during ordinary browsing.

Infrastructure providers may use technologies necessary to deliver or protect their services. Their use of such technologies is subject to their own practices and documentation.

This information may change if new features or third-party services are added.

5. How information is used

Information received through email is used to respond to messages and enquiries.

Technical information processed by hosting infrastructure may be used for delivering, maintaining and protecting the website.

6. Data retention

Email correspondence is retained only for as long as reasonably necessary to deal with the enquiry and for any relevant administrative or legal requirements.

Technical log retention is managed by the infrastructure providers and may be subject to their own retention policies.

7. Your privacy rights

Depending on the circumstances and applicable data protection law, you may have rights concerning personal information relating to you, including rights to access, correct, erase or restrict certain processing, and to object to certain uses.

To make a privacy enquiry or request, contact:

chonang@chonang.com.np

You also have the right to raise a concern with the UK Information Commissioner's Office (ICO) where applicable:

https://ico.org.uk/

8. External websites

This website may contain links to external websites. I am not responsible for the privacy practices or content of websites operated by other organisations. Please consult their own privacy policies.

9. Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated when the website's features, services or privacy practices change. The latest revision date will be shown at the top of this page.
